# Website Development Plan

## Company Overview
- The company creates enterprise user management systems and ERP products for organizations that need software to support complex business operations.
- **Enterprise user management systems** can be presented around the needs they address, such as managing user accounts, roles and permissions, authentication, and controlled access to business applications. Confirm the exact capabilities of the company's products before publishing specific claims.
- **ERP products** help organizations bring business workflows and information together. Describe the actual modules, integrations, industries, and customer outcomes supported by the company's products once those details are confirmed.
- The website should communicate the company's expertise, explain its products in terms of customer needs, and make it easy for prospective customers to start a conversation.

## Project Overview
- **Purpose and goals**: Establish the company's online presence, introduce its enterprise user management systems and ERP products, showcase product interfaces and capabilities, and provide a clear way for prospective customers to make inquiries.
- **Target audience**: Organizations evaluating enterprise software, including business and technology decision-makers seeking user management, access control, or ERP solutions. Refine audience segments and industries as more company details are added.
- **Content to gather**: Company history, product names, supported capabilities, target industries, customer examples, differentiators, and approved contact details.

## Requirements
- **Functional requirements**:
	- Present the company and its products and services.
	- Include product or solution content that explains relevant capabilities and customer benefits.
	- Provide a portfolio or product showcase with image carousels for application screenshots.
	- Provide contact information and an inquiry path for prospective customers.
	- Add case studies or customer stories when approved content is available.
- **Non-functional requirements**:
	- Work across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.
	- Load efficiently, including pages containing product screenshots.
	- Meet accessibility expectations, including keyboard-operable carousel controls and descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
	- Use secure, privacy-conscious handling for any inquiry form and submitted data.

## Design
- Create and review wireframes for the homepage, product or solutions pages, portfolio/showcase, and contact section or page.
- Define the visual direction, color palette, typography, and image treatment to reflect the company's enterprise software focus.
- Design carousels with clear previous/next controls, indicators where useful, responsive image sizing, and accessible labels. Avoid relying on automatic motion for essential content.

## Development
- **Technology stack**: Choose based on the existing repository, hosting needs, content-editing requirements, and team preferences; record the decision before implementation.
- Set up the development environment, version control workflow, and deployment process.
- Implement the responsive company overview, product content, screenshot showcase/carousels, and contact path.
- Keep product claims and content easy to review and update as additional company details are gathered.
